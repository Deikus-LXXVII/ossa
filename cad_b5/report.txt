Placement decisions:
  • Board stays at Y=150, Z=65. Lowering it to 55 would drop the wedge
    and the taller 420 would hit the board.
  • Front 360 is raised 10 mm (Z 10..65) so fan tops line up with the
    board bottom. The 10 mm plinth is an intake gap.
  • In the old 360 slot the 420 already intersects the board (~0.8 mm).
    It is 17 mm taller and 10 mm thicker. Moved back and up:
      420 at Y 353..418, Z 106..244
    giving 15 mm shortest distance to the board back and 20 mm to the PSU top.
  • PSU stays 150 along X, 210 along Y, 86 tall, but is shifted back
    to Y 243..453 so the IEC inlet at Y=453
    sits behind the 25 mm exhaust and stays reachable from the rear.
    Cables come out at the front of the PSU, under the board.
  • Front I/O pod is reserved on the right (X 212..302, Y 8..98, Z 8..72),
    in the width freed by the narrower 360 (394 vs 458) and the shallower
    front (starts at Y=29 vs 12).

Issues / watch-outs:
  • The swap is possible, but the rear 420 no longer fits in the old 360
    pocket. The case is deeper (~460 vs ~405) because the 420 had to
    move back and the PSU had to follow so the IEC stays reachable.
  • 20 mm between PSU top and 420 is better than 4 mm, but a top-mounted
    PSU fan is still tight. Prefer a bottom intake (vented base) or a
    rear/side intake. The IEC is at Y=447, below the 420's exhaust.
  • 360 sits on a 10 mm plinth; cut a grille in it if the 360 intakes down,
    or blow it up into the board/GPU (similar chimney to B4, slightly more
    front space).
  • Blow the 420 backward, away from the board (Y 418..443 is free).
  • CPU tubes must go around or over the board to the rear 420. Same-side
    or over-the-top routes should be used; fittings on one end of the 420,
    not opposite corners.
  • GPU tubes to the front 360 are short and stay in front of the board.
    Same-side fittings are easiest; opposite-side also likely fine.
  • I/O pod is on the right, next to JUSB3 / JUSBC1 / JFP1. Route the
    USB 3 and USB-C cables around the 360's right end, not through it.
    Typical 500 mm USB 3 and USB-C cables reach with plenty of slack.
    JAUD1 sits on the opposite (left) edge: a stock 500 mm HD-audio cable is
    short, so plan a 700 mm front-audio extension along the front lip.
  • Rear I/O of the board is still at the top edge (~Y 366, Z 281),
    45° up-back, above the 420 (top Z=244).
  • The organic frame v1 is a volume sketch: skiff base, left/right rails,
    filleted I/O pod, spine, 360 plinth and 420 cradles. Split joints and
    real hollowing / grilles come later.
Frame does not unexpectedly intersect the iron.
