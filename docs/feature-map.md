# Feature map: what the case has to do, and what proves it

Written on 17 Nov 2026. One row per thing the owner will do with the case or expect of it. "Proved in the model" means a script fails when it is not so. "Proved on the hardware" means the owner tried a printed gauge or part. A row without proof is a gap, not a promise.

Use it two ways: as the definition of done for a revision (no row may get worse), and as the list of what still has to be proved before printing in ABS.

## Budgets

What the owner pays for, recorded in `cad_b6/budgets.json` and held by `cad_b6/check_budgets.py` (exit 1 on a FAIL; `cad_b6/test_budgets.py` proves each budget fails when exceeded, when its input is missing, when a figure falls under its floor (a count under its record, a mass or a volume more than 5 % under it), and when the suite's record is of other code, of other packages or another Python, short or half written). A figure may grow by its margin; a budget is lowered with `--write` in a commit of its own, and `--write` refuses a figure past a hard limit, and one under its floor unless `--accept-lower` says the saving is meant.

| Feature | Proved in the model | Proved on the hardware | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| The skeleton prints from the black ABS on hand | `check_budgets.py`: ABS at 4 walls / 20 % 1534.6 g, ratchet 1565.3 g (+ 2 %), floor 1457.9 g (- 5 %), hard limit 1800 g (90 % of the 2 kg in `hw.ABS_STOCK_G`) | none | 265.4 g (14.7 %) left under the hard limit; supports and rejects are not counted; the 4 walls / 20 % formula is an estimate, not a slicer's figure |
| The organic layer prints from the PETG ordered | `check_budgets.py`: 2218.7 cm3 (2818 g solid, `main` 2431 g and `rear_panel` 387 g) with the rear panel, ratchet + 2 %, floor - 5 %, hard limit 3600 g solid (90 % of 4 kg) | none | solid volume, not the slicer's; supports not counted |
| The checks run often enough to be used | `check_budgets.py`: the fast `test_checks.py` gated on six counts, each limited to the larger of + 1 and + 10 % rounded down: frame builds 9 (limit 10), `run_checks` calls 125 (limit 137), cable routings 13 (14), cable evaluations 6 (7), zone_access runs 8 (9), drive_access maps 3 (4); a count under its record fails (the suite did less work). Its wall time (347.3 s recorded, 6 workers) warns at its margin of 0.25. The record must be of the working tree's code (its digest) and of the Python and package versions installed now, and hold the 328 cases of `EXPECTED_CASES` | none | wall time depends on the machine's load; counts do not see a slower single operation; on a small count + 1 is more than 10 % (3 drive_access maps may become 4) |

Cooling: the direction of each fan is decided, the air flow is not computed. Noise and vibration. Dust.
