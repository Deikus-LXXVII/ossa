Issues / watch-outs:
  • 420 sits on the base (Z=0). If it intakes from below, the base needs a grille
    and short feet. If it blows up, that air goes into the PSU and the underside
    of the board / GPU (86 mm clearance from the 420 to the GPU).
  • PSU top is only ~4 mm under the 360. A top-mounted PSU fan would be starved.
    Use a bottom intake (vented base + feet) or a rear/side intake; IEC is at Y=400.
  • 360 exhaust needs a clear 25 mm behind it (Y 370..395). The back of the frame
    is left open. Blow the 360 backward, away from the board.
  • Board rear I/O is at the top edge (~Y 366, Z 281), facing up and back at 45°.
    The 360 (top Z=211) does not block it, but cables come in from above.
  • GPU already overhangs the board by 18 mm each side. A PCIe 5.0 riser would
    let it move off the board entirely (not modelled here).
  • GPU tubes must go around the board to reach the 360. Same-side ports pass;
    opposite-side ports fail the 30% slack check on 450 mm tubes.
  • Board-to-360 clearance is tight (11.4 mm). Do not thicken
    the spine past 5 mm without checking.
  • Split joints need fasteners / heat-set inserts later; this is only a volume check.
Frame does not unexpectedly intersect the iron.
